# Chronos — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [FILL IN THE DATE YOU PUBLISH]_

Chronos is built around a simple idea: **your data is yours, and it stays with
you.** Chronos has no servers of its own and no account to sign up for. This
policy explains exactly what the app touches and where it goes.

## The short version

- Chronos does **not** collect, sell, or share your personal data with the
  developer or any third party.
- There are **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.**
- Your calendar events and tasks live in **your own Apple Calendar and Apple
  Reminders** — Chronos reads and writes them with your permission but never
  copies them anywhere else.
- Everything else Chronos creates (momentum, habits, journal, focus history,
  settings) is stored **on your device**, and — only if you turn it on —
  synced through **your own private iCloud**.

## What Chronos accesses, and why

**Apple Calendar & Reminders (required for the app to work).**
With your permission, Chronos reads and writes your calendar events (as time
blocks) and reminders (as tasks). This information stays in Apple's Calendar and
Reminders stores on your device and your iCloud, governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Chronos does not
transmit it to the developer.

**On-device app data.**
Data you create inside Chronos — habits and goals, journal entries, the focus
log, momentum history, category tags, your planning profile from calibration,
and your settings — is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage.

**Notifications (optional).**
If you allow notifications, Chronos schedules **local** reminders (start-of-block
nudges, morning/evening rituals, habit reminders) on your device. These are not
sent through any server.

**On-device AI Coach (optional, capable devices only).**
On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the Coach runs on Apple's
**on-device** language model. Your schedule and stats are used locally to answer
you; **nothing is sent off the device** for this feature.

## iCloud sync (optional — off by default)

If you enable iCloud sync, Chronos stores a copy of its on-device data (habits,
goals, journal, momentum, focus log, planning profile, routines, tags, and
settings) in **your own private iCloud database** so it appears on your other
Apple devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit and your personal iCloud account; the
developer cannot see it. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how iCloud handles data.

## Friends & leaderboards (optional — off by default)

If you turn on the social features, a small amount of information you choose to
share — the display name you set, an optional status (emoji + short line), your
current block's title, and coarse stats like your streak, level, and focus
minutes — is written to a **shared iCloud (CloudKit public) area** so friends you
connect with can see your presence, and to **Game Center** for optional
leaderboards. You control this: it is off until you enable it, you pick your
display name and status, and you can turn it off at any time. Do not put anything
private in your display name or status.

## Connecting a school (optional)

If you connect a school learning system (e.g. Canvas or Schoology), you provide
an access token that Chronos uses to fetch **your** assignments directly from
**your school's** servers and turn them into reminders on your device. That
request goes to your school, not to the developer, and the token is stored only
on your device.

## Data the developer receives

**None.** Chronos has no backend operated by the developer. The developer does
not receive your calendar, tasks, notes, stats, location, contacts, or any
usage analytics. If Apple provides the developer with anonymous, aggregate App
Store statistics (downloads, crash counts), that comes from Apple and is not
linked to you.

## Children

Chronos does not knowingly collect personal information from children and
requires no account. It is rated for general audiences.

## Your choices

- Grant or revoke Calendar, Reminders, and Notifications access anytime in
  **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security** (or the app's permission prompts).
- Turn iCloud sync and social features on or off in **Chronos → Settings**.
- Deleting the app removes its on-device data. Data already written to your
  Apple Calendar/Reminders and your iCloud remains yours and can be managed in
  the Apple apps and iCloud settings.
